Problem

Revenue, installment plans, commissions and attendance lived in separate Excel files. Month-end reporting was slow and overdue balances slipped through.

What I built

A 14-module dashboard, then a v2 on a MySQL database with logins for admin, manager and viewer roles, browse pages for every record type, and a Power BI model.

Stack

MySQL, PHP, JavaScript and Chart.js, Power BI (DAX), Excel. Deployed on the clinic's local server with Arabic and English views.

Outcome

One source of truth for ~1,900 patients. Discount approvals and overdue installments were identified as the highest-impact decisions to support next.

Business study · Jordan · two-sided marketplace

Glow Pass: beauty membership marketplace

A monthly membership for recurring beauty services. Members pay a flat fee, partner salons are paid per visit and fill their off-peak slots, and the platform keeps the margin. I built the market research, unit economics, 12-month model and a validation plan with go/no-go gates before any money is spent.

MembersWomen 20–35 in West Amman with 2+ beauty visits a month. Plans from 25 to 75 JOD.
SalonsMid to upscale, free to join, paid every 1–2 weeks for visits that would otherwise sit empty.
Average plan price38.0 JOD
Salon payouts (members use ~80% of visits)−22.0
Payment fees and promos−1.2
Contribution per member14.8 JOD
Break-even = 400 JOD fixed costs ÷ 14.8 ≈ 27 members

Monthly net result, first 12 months

JOD · paying members under each month · scenario estimates

Profitable from month 4 at 38 members, reaching about 2,300 JOD a month at 210 members. Gate 1 needs 150 waitlist sign-ups and 10 salons agreeing to rates before launch spending starts.
